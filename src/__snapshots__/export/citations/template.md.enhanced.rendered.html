Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet (Ipsum and Dolor 2020), consectetur adipiscing elit (Amet 2019, 3). Sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt (see Tempor 2018, chap. 2, emphasis added) ut labore et dolore magna aliqua (Ipsum and Dolor 2020; Amet 2019).

Magna (2021) says ut enim ad minim veniam, and Tempor (2018, 7) agrees. Quis nostrud exercitation ullamco laboris[1] nisi ut aliquip.
